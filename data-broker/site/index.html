About This Data

This dataset is compiled from publicly available H1B visa disclosure records published by the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA).

We aggregate, clean, and repackage this data so you can use it without dealing with government PDFs and inconsistent formats. Every record includes the employer name, job title, annual salary, work location, and filing year.
