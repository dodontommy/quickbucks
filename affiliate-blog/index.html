Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before choosing a VPN

Is it safe to use a free VPN?
Free VPNs often come with serious drawbacks: data caps, slow speeds, and privacy risks. Many free VPNs log and sell your data to third parties. A 2021 study by CSIRO found that 38% of free Android VPNs contained tracking libraries. We strongly recommend using a reputable paid VPN with a proven no-logs policy — the cost is roughly the same as a cup of coffee per month, and your privacy is worth it.
Which VPN is fastest in 2026?
ExpressVPN consistently ranks as the fastest VPN in our speed tests, with an average speed loss of just 8%. NordVPN is a close second at 11% using its NordLynx protocol. Both use modern, optimized protocols (Lightway and WireGuard-based respectively) that minimize encryption overhead. For most users, the difference between the two is imperceptible in real-world browsing and streaming.
Can a VPN unblock Netflix?
Yes — premium VPNs can reliably unblock Netflix and other streaming services. In our testing, ExpressVPN unblocks 20+ Netflix libraries including US, UK, Japan, Canada, Australia, and Germany. NordVPN and Surfshark also consistently unblock major libraries. Free VPNs almost never work with Netflix, as the streaming service actively blocks known free VPN IP addresses.
Do VPNs keep logs of my activity?
All five VPNs on this list have a strict no-logs policy that has been independently audited by third-party firms such as Deloitte, PwC, and KPMG. Private Internet Access has even proven its no-logs policy in court — when served with a subpoena, the company could not provide user data because they didn't have any. Always check a VPN's privacy policy and audit history before purchasing. Avoid VPNs based in countries that are part of the 5/9/14 Eyes surveillance alliances.
How much should I pay for a VPN?
Quality VPNs range from $2.19 to $13 per month depending on the plan length. Long-term plans (2-3 years) offer the best value, typically $2-5 per month. Monthly plans are significantly more expensive ($11-13/mo) and are best only for short-term needs. We recommend signing up for a long-term plan during seasonal sales (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, New Year) when prices drop 70-80% off the monthly rate. Most services offer a 30-day money-back guarantee, so you can try risk-free.
Will a VPN slow down my internet?
Every VPN will cause some speed reduction due to encryption overhead, but the best modern VPNs minimize this to nearly imperceptible levels. ExpressVPN and NordVPN typically cause only 8-11% speed loss thanks to their optimized protocols. On a 500 Mbps connection, you'll still get 440-460 Mbps — more than enough for 4K streaming, gaming, and video calls. Older VPN protocols (OpenVPN) can cause 30-50% speed loss, which is why we recommend services using WireGuard or proprietary lightweight protocols.
